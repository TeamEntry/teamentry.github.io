Privacy Policy

Applies to the RemoteScreen apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Effective November 16, 2026.

  • TeamEntry does not collect personal data through this app.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your screen and input travel encrypted only between your Mac and your devices. They never pass through or get stored on TeamEntry servers.

1. Information the app handles

Screen and input

Your Mac's screen and your touch and keyboard input travel directly between the Mac and the devices you paired. Connections use your Tailscale network or a local network you allowed, encrypted with TLS 1.3 over QUIC. TeamEntry never receives or stores this content.

Stored only on your devices

The app keeps the following on your devices only. It is removed when you delete the app.

  • The list, names and certificate fingerprints of paired Macs and devices
  • A device key created in the Secure Enclave and the Mac's server certificate. Both stay in the Keychain and can't be exported.
  • App settings such as quality and network
  • Daily Wi-Fi and cellular data usage
  • Ask AI chats from the Guide tab

Ask AI

Answers in the Guide tab are generated on device by Apple Intelligence. Your questions and answers are not sent to TeamEntry.

2. Permissions

  • Camera (iPhone, iPad): Used only to scan the pairing QR code on your Mac. Nothing is saved or sent.
  • Face ID (iPhone, iPad): Confirms it's you before the device key is used on each connection. The app only receives the result.
  • Local Network: Finds and connects to Macs and devices on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Screen & System Audio Recording (Mac): Sends the screen to your paired devices.
  • Accessibility (Mac): Delivers touch and keyboard input from your paired devices to the Mac.

You can turn off any permission in Settings at any time. Only the features that need it stop working.

3. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or crash reporting.

  • Tailscale is a separate service you install and sign in to yourself. Information it handles is covered by the Tailscale Privacy Policy.
  • App Store downloads and crash reports you choose to share are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. TeamEntry only sees anonymous statistics from Apple.

4. Retention and deletion

TeamEntry keeps no user data. Data on your devices is removed when you delete a Mac or a chat in the app, or when you delete the app.

5. Children's privacy

The app collects no one's personal data, including children's.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If how we handle data changes, we'll also note it in the app's update notes.
