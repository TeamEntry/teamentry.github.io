Getting started

  1. Download Kiku from the App Store and open it. The first-run guide walks you through these steps.
  2. In Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard, choose Kiku and turn on the languages you use.
  3. For key sounds and haptics, tap Kiku and turn on Allow Full Access.
  4. In any text field, touch and hold the globe key and choose Kiku.
  5. For saved phrases, clipboard, kaomoji and notes, also add Kiku Pocket there.

Frequently asked questions

Kiku doesn’t appear in the keyboard list

Open the app once, then open Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard again. If it’s still missing, restart your iPhone.

I don’t hear key sounds or feel haptics

Check that Allow Full Access is on for the Kiku keyboard and that your iPhone isn’t in Silent mode. Also check Sound and Haptics in Customize in the app.

Themes I made in the app don’t show on the keyboard

The keyboard needs Allow Full Access to load themes and sounds from the app. If it still doesn’t change, switch to another keyboard and back to Kiku.

Is what I type stored or sent anywhere?

No. Kiku has no server and never stores, logs or sends what you type. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I send a theme to a friend?

Tap Share on a theme to send a card image or a theme file. Opening the theme file adds it to Kiku. Recorded or imported sounds aren’t included in theme files.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget and choose Kiku. Widgets follow your current keyboard theme.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting Kiku removes all of its themes, sounds and settings.
