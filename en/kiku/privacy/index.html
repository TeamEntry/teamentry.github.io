Privacy Policy

Applies to the Kiku iPhone app and keyboards. Effective November 27, 2026.

  • Kiku does not collect personal data.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Kiku never stores, logs or sends anything you type with the Kiku keyboard.

1. Information the app handles

What you type

Nothing you type with the Kiku keyboard is stored, logged or sent off your device. Kiku has no server.

Stored only on your device

Your style and settings, themes, sounds and haptic patterns, and Kiku Pocket phrases, clipboard items, kaomoji and notes are saved only in the app’s private space on your device (App Group). Deleting the app deletes them.

Kiku Pocket clipboard history

Clipboard history is off by default. If you turn it on in the Kiku app, Kiku Pocket saves only newly copied text on this device when you open its Clipboard tab. It keeps at most 10 items for one hour and skips text longer than 2,000 characters. When it is off, the keyboard does not read the clipboard.

2. Permissions

  • Full Access: Used only so the keyboard can play key sounds and haptics, load the themes and sounds you made in the app, and read and save Kiku Pocket phrases, kaomoji, notes and the clipboard history you turned on, all on this device. Even with Full Access, the keyboard sends nothing over the internet.
  • Microphone: Used only in Sound Studio when you tap Record. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Photos: Only the photo you pick as a keyboard background is read.

You can turn off any permission in Settings at any time. Only the features that need it stop working.

3. Sharing

A theme card image or theme file is sent only when you share it, to the app you choose (Messages, AirDrop, etc.).

4. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or crash reporting. App Store downloads and crash reports you choose to share are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. TeamEntry only sees anonymous statistics from Apple.

5. Retention and deletion

TeamEntry keeps no user data. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app.

6. Children’s privacy

The app collects no one’s personal data, including children’s.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the new version here with a new effective date.
