Getting started

  1. Install and open the Screen Sharing Mac app on the Mac you want to see.
  2. On the Mac, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and allow Screen & System Audio Recording and Accessibility.
  3. Connect both devices to the same Tailscale network. If you only use the same Wi-Fi, turn on Accept local network connections in the Mac app's settings.
  4. Click Add Device in the Mac menu bar to show a QR code, then scan it with the iPhone or iPad app to pair.
  5. From then on, tap your Mac in the list and confirm with Face ID.

No Mac nearby? Tap Explore with a Demo Mac on the first screen to try every control.

Frequently asked questions

My iPhone can't connect to my Mac

Please check the following in order.

  • The Mac is awake and its menu bar status shows Waiting
  • If you use Tailscale, it's on for both devices and both are signed in to the same account
  • If you set your own Tailscale ACLs, UDP port 47100 on the Mac is allowed
  • If you connect over Wi-Fi, Accept local network connections is on and this device's Local network switch is on in Paired devices
Do I need Tailscale?

Not if you only use the same Wi-Fi. With Accept local network connections on, you can pair and connect without Tailscale. To reach your Mac from another network, you need Tailscale.

I can see the screen, but clicks and typing don't work

On the Mac, check that Screen Sharing is on in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility. If it already is, turn the switch off and on again, then reopen the Mac app.

I see a warning that the certificate fingerprint changed

This appears when the Mac's server key changed or another Mac is using the same address. If it's your Mac, remove it on your iPhone and pair again.

How much cellular data does it use?

Each quality option in Settings shows an estimate per hour. The default, Automatic, lowers resolution and frame rate on cellular and in Low Data Mode. On the same Wi-Fi, it doesn't use your plan's data.

Is my screen stored anywhere?

No. Your screen and input travel encrypted only between your Mac and your devices, and never pass through TeamEntry servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I disconnect or remove a paired device?

In the Mac menu bar, use Disconnect or Disconnect All for devices viewing now. Choose Remove Pairing in Paired devices to cut a device off right away. On iPhone or iPad, use Delete This Computer on that Mac.
